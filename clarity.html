UX/UI DESIGN PROJECT

CLARITY

A Finance Facilitator App for your Teenager

AI-powered financial education app that helps teenagers build healthy budgeting and spending habits.

Role Product Designer Lead
Team Ema Nova & Alanah Little
Tools Figma, FigJam, AI
Primary User Group Teenagers beginning to build healthy money habits.

Project Objective

As young adults begin managing their own finances, many struggle with budgeting, tracking subscriptions, and building healthy financial habits. This project aims to design an intuitive digital experience that helps teenagers develop financial confidence through budgeting, savings, personalized insights, and AI-powered guidance.

Our Mission

To empower teenagers to develop healthy financial habits early by making money management simple, visual, and engaging.

Main Question

How might we help teenagers understand and manage their finances without overwhelming them?

The Problem

Teens often struggle to understand budgeting, manage subscriptions, build savings habits, and create realistic budgets. As a result, many enter adulthood without the financial knowledge and confidence needed to make informed decisions.

The Solution

Clarity empowers teens to build strong financial habits with budgeting tools, savings goals, subscription management, spending insights, AI-powered guidance, and an optional Parent Mode for encouragement and support.

Design Process

Milestone 1: Understanding User Needs & Pain Points

Primary Research

Conducted user interviews and usability testing with teenagers to understand their financial habits, challenges, and expectations when managing money.

Research focused on:

• Budgeting difficulties
• Spending awareness
• Subscription management
• Savings goals

Findings showed that teens need a simple, visual, and approachable way to build financial confidence.

Secondary Research

Conducted competitive analysis of existing financial platforms such as Mint, YNAB, and Copilot.

Research showed many current solutions are designed for adults with existing financial knowledge, making them overwhelming for younger users.

Key insights:

• Simplicity improves engagement
• Visual feedback improves understanding
• Personalized guidance increases confidence

Key Features

Milestone 2: Translating Flow Into Experience

Budget Tracker

Track spending habits, monitor monthly budgets, and help teenagers understand where their money goes.

Subscription Manager

Manage recurring subscriptions, receive reminders, and prevent unexpected spending.

Savings Goal Tracker

Create personal savings goals and visualize progress toward financial milestones.

Monthly Spending Report

Provide visual summaries of spending patterns to help teenagers make informed financial decisions.

Carl AI Assistant

Provides financial guidance, budget tips, and personalized recommendations.

Parent Mode

Allows parents to monitor their teenager's financial activity, provide guidance, and encourage healthy money habits.

Design Refinement

Milestone 3: Refining Experience

Adaptive Notification System

Clarity uses adaptive notification strategies inspired by Multi-Armed Bandit algorithms to provide more relevant financial reminders.

Instead of sending constant notifications, the system learns user preferences and identifies the best timing and type of message.

Conclusion

Through research and design exploration, Clarity was developed to address the challenges teenagers face when learning to manage their finances.

The findings highlighted the importance of creating a financial experience that is simple, visual, and supportive rather than overwhelming.

By combining budgeting tools, spending insights, savings goals, subscription management, and personalized guidance, Clarity aims to help young users build financial awareness and confidence.
